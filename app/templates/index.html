Welcome to the Listen Here! environment for tool-assisted close listening to aligned performance collections. A publicly accessible version of this tool will be available shortly. In the meantime, have a look at the tool's alignment workflow (based on SyncToolbox) to begin preparing your own aligned performance collections.

The tool and its associated Web-native digital musicology environment are described in this publication. A further publication with extended detail is under review.

The tool's open-source codebase is written in JavaScript and Python Flask, licensed under AGPL-3.0, developed within Signature Sound Vienna, a research project funded by the FWF – Austrian Science Fund (P 34664-G). Hosting is provided by the Department of Music Acoustics – Wiener Klangstil at the mdw – University of Music and Performing Arts Vienna.
